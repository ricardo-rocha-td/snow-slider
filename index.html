Snow Slider Puzzles

Slide a penguin across the ice and find the way to the flag.

iPhone and iPad App Store Download Android phones and tablets Google Play Get it

On ice there's no stopping halfway: every swipe sends your penguin gliding until something gets in the way. The trick is to use it. Plan each slide, line up the next one, and reach the flag in as few moves as you can.

Screenshots

What's inside

210 puzzles, 13 mountains

Start with the basics on Frost Peak, then climb. Each mountain brings something new: ramps, snowbanks, portals, cracked ice, curling stones and polar bears.

Stars and cosmetics

Finish within par for three stars. Stars open new mountains and dress your penguin, from a bobble beanie to a ram's skull helmet.

Never stuck for good

Hints show the way from wherever you are and never cost stars. Undo and restart are a tap away.

Play anywhere

No internet, no account, no timers. Your progress follows you between iPhone and iPad through iCloud.
