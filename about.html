About

A penguin, a lot of ice, and two people.

Hi, I'm Ricardo. I've been a software engineer for 8 years, and Snow Slider is the game I build in my own time, as a solo developer: the levels, the code, the art and the sounds (every sound effect is made in code), in Godot.

It started with one simple idea: on ice, you can't stop halfway. From there it grew mountain by mountain, each one adding a new thing to slide into, bounce off or push around, until a penguin had 13 mountains to climb.

I didn't make it alone, though. My girlfriend, Karla, has played every version of it: she tries the new levels first, tells me when one is too hard or not fun yet, and has shaped more of the game than anyone. If a level feels just right, she probably had a hand in it.

Thank you for playing. If you'd like to say hello, report a bug or share a solve, I'd love to hear from you.
