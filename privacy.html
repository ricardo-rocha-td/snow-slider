Snow Slider Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: Snow Slider collects nothing. Your progress stays on your phone and the game never goes online.

Snow Slider Puzzles ("Snow Slider", "the game") is made by Spice Octopus Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it, on iPhone and on Android.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (stars, levels completed, hats, hints left and which hints you've revealed) and your settings on your device only. We never receive it. It is deleted when you delete the game, and "Reset progress" in the settings clears most of it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no purchases, and it makes no internet connections. We collect no personal data of any kind.

Children

Snow Slider isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything (for example crash reports, or with optional ads), we'll update this page, its date above and the stores' privacy labels before that version is released.
