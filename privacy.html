Snow Slider Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: Snow Slider collects nothing. Your progress stays on your device and, on iPhone and iPad, in your own iCloud. We never see it.

Snow Slider Puzzles ("Snow Slider", "the game") is made by Spice Octopus Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it, on iPhone and on Android.

Where your progress is kept

The game saves your progress (stars, levels completed, hats, hints left and which hints you've revealed) and your settings on your device. On iPhone and iPad, your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, through Apple's iCloud, so it comes back if you install the game again and is shared by your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We never receive it and can't see it. It's deleted from a device when you delete the game there; you can remove the iCloud copy in your iPhone's Settings (your name › iCloud). Each install's copy is labelled with a random number the game makes up for it, so your devices' copies don't overwrite each other; it says nothing about you or your device. "Reset progress" in the settings clears most of it, on every device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no purchases, and it contacts no server of ours or anyone else's (only iCloud, as above). We collect no personal data of any kind.

Now and then, after a good moment like finishing a mountain, the game asks the App Store or Google Play to show its own "rate this app" prompt. The store decides whether it appears, and any rating or review you give goes to the store under its own privacy policy; the game sends nothing with it and never learns what you chose.

Children

Snow Slider isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything (for example crash reports, or with optional ads), we'll update this page, its date above and the stores' privacy labels before that version is released.
