Snow Slider Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: we collect nothing. Your progress stays on your phone. The optional rewarded ads are run by Google, and hint packs are sold by Apple or Google, as described below.

Snow Slider Puzzles ("Snow Slider", "the game") is made by Spice Octopus Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (stars, levels completed, hats, hints left and which hints you've revealed) and your settings on your device only. We never receive it. It is deleted when you delete the game, and "Reset progress" in the settings clears most of it. The game has no analytics and no accounts.

Rewarded ads

You can watch an ad for free hints. Ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one, and are run by Google AdMob. Google may collect your IP address, information about your device and the ad, and (where you allow it) your device's advertising ID, to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later from "Ad privacy" in the game's settings. On iPhone, the advertising ID is used only if you allow it when iOS asks; you get the same hints either way. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Hint packs

Hint packs are optional purchases, handled entirely by the App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android, under Apple's and Google's privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a transaction or order number, kept on your device so each purchase is paid out once.

Children

Snow Slider isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new (for example crash reports), we'll update this page, its date above and the store's privacy labels before that version is released.
