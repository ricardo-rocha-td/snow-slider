Snow Slider Puzzles: Support

Slide a penguin across the ice and find the way to the flag.

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Write to polvinhosjacintos@gmail.com. Please say which phone you have and, for a level, which mountain and level number it is.

How do I play?

Swipe to slide. The penguin keeps going until something stops it, so plan where each slide ends. Reach the flag to finish the level. Finish in as few moves as the best way (par) for three stars.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap the lightbulb for a hint: it shows the next moves from wherever the penguin is, and never costs stars. You get hints for completing levels. You can also undo your last slide or restart. The book button explains every object you've found.

How do I open the next mountain?

Each mountain opens once you've earned enough stars on the one before it. Going back to improve old levels to three stars counts too.

Where are my hats?

Tap the penguin button on the mountain map. Hats are earned with stars across every mountain, never bought.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your phone automatically after every level. It isn't stored online, so it stays on that phone and goes if you delete the game.
